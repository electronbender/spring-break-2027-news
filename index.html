    Spring Break 2027 news FAQ

    When is spring break 2027?

    Most colleges break between late February and early April. Because Easter is March 28, 2027, the peak window is March 6–28, and the busiest weeks are projected to start March 8 and March 15. Check your own school's calendar on springbreak.com.

    Where is everyone going in 2027?

    Early group bookings put Cancún at #1, then Punta Cana, Nassau, South Padre Island and Cabo San Lucas. Miami, Las Vegas, Lake Havasu, Panama City Beach and Fort Lauderdale round out the top 10.

    How much does a 2027 package cost?

    Early 2027 all-inclusive air-and-hotel prices range from about $749 per person (select Riviera Maya resorts) to $2,491 (top-tier Punta Cana resorts). Cabo averages around $1,249 and Nassau about $1,398.

    Can I drink on the beach?

    Not in several Florida spots. Panama City Beach bans alcohol on the sand all of March, and Fort Lauderdale bans alcohol, coolers, tents and amplified music on the beach. Miami Beach runs DUI checkpoints, bag checks and early liquor-store curfews.
